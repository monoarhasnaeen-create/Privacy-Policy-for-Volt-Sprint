Privacy Policy for Volt Sprint

Thank you for playing Volt Sprint. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Volt Sprint mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account & Authentication Information

Volt Sprint uses Google Sign-In (via Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication) to identify your account and keep your game progress safe. When you sign in, we receive and store:

  • Your unique Firebase User ID (userId)
  • Your public Google display name (used as your in-game runner profile name)

b. Gameplay & Cloud Sync Data

To synchronize your progress across sessions and devices via Cloud Firestore, we store:

  • Virtual Coin balance
  • Pulse Shield inventory count
  • High score
  • Daily Gift streak day and last claim timestamp

c. 16-Character Backup & Recovery Code

Each player profile is assigned a randomly generated 16-character alphanumeric backup code. This code maps to a recovery snapshot containing only your gameplay progress (coins, shields, high score, and daily gift streak). It does not store personal contact information such as email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses. Keep your backup code confidential, as anyone with the code can restore that progress snapshot.

d. In-App Purchase Records (Google Play Billing)

All virtual coin packs and shield power-up purchases are processed strictly through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials. When a purchase is completed, we record non-financial transaction metadata in your private purchase ledger—such as the product ID, item title, reward quantity, display price, Google Play Order ID, and purchase token—to verify delivery and prevent duplicate crediting.

2. Virtual Currency & Items

Virtual Coins and Pulse Shields purchased or earned inside Volt Sprint are digital in-game items only. They have no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed for cash, and cannot be transferred or sold outside the game.

3. Permissions Used

Volt Sprint follows the principle of least privilege and requests only:

  • INTERNET: Required for Google Sign-In, Cloud Firestore synchronization, account recovery, and Google Play Billing.

Volt Sprint does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or external file storage.

4. Third-Party Services

Volt Sprint relies on trusted Google services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Data Security & Retention

All player data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and protected by Firebase Authentication and strict owner-isolated Firestore Security Rules so that only your authenticated account can read or modify your private profile and purchase history. We retain your game data for as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion.

6. Data Deletion Requests

You may stop using the app at any time. If you wish to permanently delete your Volt Sprint cloud profile, backup recovery snapshot, and purchase ledger, please send a request to monoarhasnaeen@gmail.com with your account display name or 16-character backup code, and we will process your deletion request within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

Volt Sprint is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
